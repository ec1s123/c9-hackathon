Operator Tracker
/
Built from GRID event feeds (Sky's The Limit 2026 hackathon data). Positions are sampled whenever any player uses an ability, deals damage or gets a kill, so tracks are approximate. Missed shots are not recorded: “Operator shots” here always means shots that hit. Callout names are nearest-label estimates. Agent, ability and weapon art belongs to Riot Games and team logos to the teams; they are shown locally and are not part of this project.
